How it pays

Three steps. One cent.

01

Send an address

Paste a Celo address into Telegram. That is the whole interface.

02

It pays the toll

The data route answers 402 with a price. TollScout signs a stablecoin payment locally and retries, within a one-cent ceiling.

03

You get the receipt

A short report, plus an itemised receipt of what was bought and what it cost.

Spend cap

An agent with a wallet needs a ceiling.

TollScout spends from its own wallet, never yours, and every call carries a hard cap. A route that asks for more is not paid automatically.
